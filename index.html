The Studio

Small studio,
big bounces

Siegward Games is one desk, one developer, and an unreasonable amount of thought about how a ball should wobble. I make lightweight, playful games that live on your desktop instead of staying stuck in a window.

Everything here is built, tested and tweaked by one person, so updates land when they're ready. The goal stays the same: small games that feel good to have around every day.

Current Project

Desktop
Bouncer

In development. Features and visuals may change.

Desktop Bouncer is a small overlay game where three ball characters move in on your desktop. They bounce around your windows and react to what you're doing with squishy, hand-tuned physics. It's still in active development, so anything you see here may change.

Lives on your desktop A real overlay, not a window. The balls sit on top of your workspace without stealing focus or blocking clicks.
Squishy real-time physics Bouncing, rolling, squash and stretch, with collisions tuned until they felt right.
Three little personalities Red is dramatic, Blue is way too excited about everything, and Green mostly wants a nap.
Light on your PC Small footprint and low CPU use. Made to run quietly in the background all day.

The base game will be free. Optional cosmetic extras may come later.
